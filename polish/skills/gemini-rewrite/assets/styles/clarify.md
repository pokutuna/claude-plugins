Make the text easier to understand.

- Fix grammar, typos, and awkward phrasing.
- Split sentences that pack conditions, actions, reasons, and results together. State omitted subjects and objects when the reader would have to guess them.
- Improve the flow between sentences and paragraphs. Reorder sentences within a paragraph when it helps.
- Replace vague wording, undefined jargon, and metaphors that hide the technical meaning with concrete wording.
- The text may get longer if that makes the meaning clear.
